# India Trader Budget Manager

Run `python -m india_trader budget-manager --budget INR --write` after `$india-trader-exit` creates `trader/plan.json` and, if desired, `$india-trader-time-machine` replays it. The command reads a paper exit plan and optional `trader/config.json`, `trader/prices.json`, and `trader/portfolio.json`. Start a config from `examples/BUDGET_CONFIG_INDIA.json`; its numbers are hypothetical paper defaults, not the user's actual funds. A real budget must be supplied by `--budget` or `budget_inr` in config. The CLI defaults to the plan path and writes nothing unless `--write` is given. `--now ISO_TIME` pins an IST replay clock and does not make a historical plan current.

The proposed whole-share size is the smallest of four limits: maximum planned loss after an explicit fee buffer; position percentage of budget; maximum single order; and budget or paper portfolio cash after the cash buffer. It shows all four share counts and the binding one. It sizes at the plan entry or a higher **dated** local quote from `trader/prices.json` that is at most 15 minutes old. `--limit` uses the plan entry; `--limit PRICE` uses that limit price. A quote without a timezone or an older quote is ignored. Slippage is applied to both the entry and stop reference. It refuses zero shares, a below-minimum order, a reward:risk under the configured minimum, a past/future-date plan, a blocked ticker, a short plan, or a target already passed. A wider stop is never moved closer to make a quantity fit.

`--write` saves `trader/orders.json` as a **paper proposal collection**; the one trade entry is compatible with `python -m india_trader risk --order trader/orders.json --as-of ISO_TIME`. A refusal writes an empty collection to avoid reusing an old proposal. `paper-ticket` can read the same one-order file after risk review, but neither command records a fill or calls a broker. A plan made ahead can be sized hypothetically; risk review blocks it as `UNTRIGGERED_PLAN`. The proposal's `paper_bracket` is a research record, not a broker request. A stop can gap past its reference, and the fee/slippage inputs do not implement the full Indian tax and charges schedule.

For weights mode, use `--weights RELIANCE=40,TCS=60` or `--targets FILE` with `{"weights":[{"ticker":"RELIANCE.NS","weight":40},...]}`. `--top N` keeps the highest weights; blocked and zero-weight names are removed; the rest are renormalized. The cash buffer is removed, overweight slices are capped and redistributed, slices below the minimum are dropped, and idle cash is shown. Without `--prices`, the result is **indicative INR allocations only**. With a dated local price for each retained ticker, it rounds to whole shares and prints no-stop paper proposals. Those proposals cannot pass paper risk review; make an individual exit plan with a stop for each ticker. `--whole-shares` explicitly requires priced input; `--cents` changes indicative rupee rounding. The calculation assumes a one-share market lot and uses no fractional quantities; verify the security's series and lot separately.

Report the budget and max loss as hypothetical until the user supplies their own numbers. Do not describe an allocation as an executable order, approval, fill, profit forecast, or exchange-verified price. The budget manager is paper-only and has no broker connection or credential handling.
