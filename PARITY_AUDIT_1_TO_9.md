# Original workflows 1–9: India paper implementation audit

Compared with the current source snapshot of [day-trader-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/day-trader-skill) on 31 October 2026. The original is a US market skill pack. This repository implements Indian cash-equity **paper** equivalents, not the original US feeds, broker connections, or Claude UI. A passing offline test establishes code behavior for a fixture, not market data coverage, fills, or profit.

| # | Original workflow and small behaviors checked | India paper implementation | Remaining external or scope limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Scanner: universe, five daily setup rules, two-stage opening-range scan, candidate cap, watchlist, filters, text/JSON, offline, partial counts | `scanner universe|daily|orb`; NSE EQ universe, BSE security master option, all five rules, staged candidate confirmation, `--max-confirm 0`, filters, watchlist handoff, offline replay, partial counts | Yahoo five-minute coverage is unofficial and BSE chart coverage is not established; NSE daily Bhavcopy is a separate final daily feed |
| 2 | News: watched symbols from scanner/plan/holdings, headlines, public filings, price moves, event tags, dedupe, seen state, dry run, offline, text/JSON | `news` uses NSE public RSS disclosures, India headlines with Google fallback, dated quote moves, tags, dedupe, state, dry run and offline replay | Public RSS is a short window and may omit direct document links; it is not a complete filing archive |
| 3 | Entry: check, bounded watch, scanner watchlist, one/five-minute ORB or level, volume threshold, first completed trigger, once-only state, saved handoff | `entry-timer check|watch|watchlist`; watch waits for the first completed bar; `.NS` and `.BO` charts accepted for paper checks | BSE timing uses an explicitly labeled NSE calendar proxy; live in-session polling and exchange reconciliation remain unverified |
| 4 | Exit: confirmed or plan-ahead entry, midpoint/range-low/price/percent/ATR stops, R or fixed target, tick rounding, flat-by, show/save, config | `exit-planner plan|show`; exact paper plan saved for Time Machine and Budget | Tick and fees need instrument-specific verification; no working broker stop or target |
| 5 | Time Machine: 10,000 seeded paths, historical rule replay, odds, verdict, chart fan/histogram, manual scenarios, save/chart | `time-machine run|chart`; seeded paths and separate historical ORB replay with source and exclusion counts | Scenario odds are not calibrated trading probabilities; Yahoo candles are unofficial |
| 6 | Budget: four binding limits, plan and weighted modes, fresh/limit price choice, min order, blocked list, reward gate, target redistribution, whole shares, output | `budget-manager` handles single plan and weighted INR targets; `examples/TARGETS_INDIA.json` gives a sample shape | Weighted targets without stops are indicative and cannot pass risk review; no actual broker cash or charge reconciliation |
| 7 | Risk: sequential order checks, date and entry window, price/risk/reward, cash/position/order/daily caps, block/duplicate, exit exemption, logged counters, report | `risk-manager` reconstructs same-day fill counts and planned loss from the log, marks existing holdings using fresh dated prices when present, and checks exact fingerprinted paper proposals | Broker cash and exchange restrictions are not independently checked; unknown future and special sessions fail closed |
| 8 | Broker: ticket display, one-ticket yes/no, decline, failed/denied result, partial or full fills, holdings, log, flatten, date controls | `broker show|approve|decline|result|fill|flatten|log|portfolio`; readable ticket/JSON, explicit paper-only decisions, partial fill accounting, recovered counters and exit proposal | Original live Alpaca/IBKR orders and working brackets are intentionally absent; all fills are local simulations at a supplied price |
| 9 | Alerts: market clock, quote, add/check/list/reset, plan stop/target/flat-by, price/percent/time/bell, rearm, holdings, source/offline, dated price export, text/JSON | `alerts clock|quote|add|check|list|reset`; IST clock, once-only state, quoted INR timestamps, saved/Yahoo source choice, output paths, config flat-by fallback, examples | Checks run only on invocation; no notification daemon or automatic close. Yahoo query1/query2 are one provider; NSE CM Market Live response has not been validated as a usable alert quote |

The adaptations above retain the workflow logic and paper handoffs while replacing US market assumptions with IST, INR and Indian symbols. The US SEC/EDGAR, Congress transaction feed, NYSE calendar, Stooq US ticker quote, and Alpaca/IBKR order APIs are not Indian data or paper-order equivalents. Earnings analysis and the insider-disclosure tracker are original workflows 10 and 11, outside this nine-workflow pass and still absent. See `QA_REPORT.md` for executed checks and limits.
